5. JOURNEY
================================================================================

Archivo: journey-organic.js (IIFE; window.__AAOrganicJourneyRuntime)

Flags homepage:

data-journey-lock-scale
data-manifest=preview-assets/journey-organic/manifest.json?v=3-hq1

Flujo real:
Scroll down
  -> scrollMetrics (sectionScroll / usableScroll / progress)
  -> frameForScroll:
       fase1 <~2.5vh: frames 0..shadeOpen(72)
       fase2: shadeOpen..last
  -> portalScaleForScroll (lockScale): push-in luego fill
  -> portalFillScale = max(vw/apW, vh/apH)*1.03 floor 1.35
  -> portalWhiteForScroll tras enterT~0.55
  -> portalChromeForScroll
  -> sticky::after --aa-portal-white #f0f0e5
  -> #shop background #F0F0E5

Funciones relevantes:
OrganicJourney, scrollMetrics, frameForScroll, portalFillScale,
portalEnterProgress, portalScaleForScroll, portalWhiteForScroll,
portalChromeForScroll, applyVisualScale, applyPortalWhite, applyPortalChrome,
renderProgress, calculateTargetScale (path non-lockScale), tick, onScroll,
onResize, onOrientation, renderStatic, fallback, shadeScrollGate

calculateTargetScale: existe; homepage lockScale usa portalFillScale en su lugar
--aa-visual-scale: forzado a 1 en lockScale
timeline.finalSwap (0.9): influye --aa-final-opacity; atenuado por white*0.35

Eventos: scroll->RAF one-shot; IntersectionObserver; resize/orientation;
stableVh en homepage para URL-bar.

staticMode si prefers-reduced-motion o conexion lenta -> poster cerrado.
