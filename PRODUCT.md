# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tourists from the United States visiting Aruba, along with travelers who want to carry the emotional memory of the island home through premium resortwear.

## Product Purpose

Arrive Aruba presents an Aruba-born clothing brand through an immersive arrival story, then guides visitors naturally into the collection. Success means the experience feels memorable and premium without obstructing shopping, particularly on mobile and in its future Shopify implementation.

## Brand Personality

Luminous, warm, and quietly luxurious. The voice prioritizes Aruba, emotional memory, and considered hospitality over generic souvenir retail.

## Anti-references

Generic tropical souvenir shops, dark cabin-like experiences, artificial neon luxury, overactive interfaces, scroll-jacking, and template-like ecommerce design.

## Design Principles

1. Let Aruba and the feeling of arrival lead every composition.
2. Use motion as a rare narrative moment, then make repeat navigation effortless.
3. Preserve Arrive Aruba typography, colors, and marks as recognizable signatures.
4. Design mobile first while retaining a calm, spacious luxury rhythm.
5. Keep commerce clear and honest without overpowering the brand story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, native scrolling, reduced-motion fallbacks, touch-friendly targets, and readable layouts below 380 px.
